Ledger Tilt

Seven lanes, one marble per $10. Each lane is an account. Run your little business for three cards, then tilt the tray and let the marbles roll to the key card.

  1. Read the card. Decide which two accounts change.
  2. Drag a marble from the supply into a lane. Pick up a marble to move it to another lane, or return it to the supply. A transfer counts as two moves.
  3. After three cards, tilt. The marbles line up against the wall, and the key card shows the number each lane should reach.

Lanes 1 to 4 are what you have and what you spent. Lanes 5 to 7 are where the money came from. The two sides always add up to the same total when the books are right.
